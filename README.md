## 🏛️ Complete Multi-Page Architecture

1. **Home (`/`)**: 16 cinematic sections, including the 0–1600ms choreography hero, manufacturer brand storytelling, 5-flacon cards, interactive signature scent finder, packaging configurator, and Tamil Nadu launch banner.
2. **About the Maison (`/about`)**: Corporate heritage, Tamil Nadu botanical terroir (Madurai, Pollachi, Nilgiris, Coromandel), mission, vision, and facility standards.
3. **Collections (`/collections`)**: Editorial showcase of all five fragrances with category filters, interactive 3D bottles, and deep-dive links.
4. **Dedicated Fragrance Pages (`/fragrances/:slug`)**:
   - `/fragrances/oud-woody-musky`
   - `/fragrances/jasmine`
   - `/fragrances/fresh-aquatic`
   - `/fragrances/citrus`
   - `/fragrances/vanilla-gourmand`
   Includes interactive cursor-reactive 3D bottle visualizer, interactive Olfactory Notes Pyramid (Top, Heart, Base), flacon metallurgy specs, and VIP launch reservation form.
5. **Manufacturing (`/manufacturing`)**: Cleanroom compounding, GC-MS fingerprinting, -4°C cryo-filtration, stainless maceration vaults, and automated bottling lines.
6. **Private Label & OEM (`/private-label`)**: B2B turn-key contract manufacturing services, custom juice formulation, bottle tooling, secondary rigid presentation boxes, and IFRA regulatory dossiers.
7. **Botanical Craftsmanship (`/craftsmanship` or `/ingredients`)**: Storytelling for Madurai Malligai, Pollachi Vanilla, Nilgiri Tea, and distillation techniques (Supercritical CO2, Cold Enfleurage, Vacuum Alembics).
8. **Bottle & Packaging Design (`/packaging`)**: 5 bespoke silhouettes, weighted magnetic caps, rigid shoulder boxes, and interactive packaging configurator.
9. **Our Process (`/process`)**: Interactive 16-week production timeline across 8 distinct manufacturing milestones.
10. **Upcoming Launch 2026 (`/upcoming-launch`)**: Dedicated Tamil Nadu pre-launch exhibition landing page, interactive bottle carousel, regional positioning (Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai), and launch reservation form with instant confetti confirmation.
11. **Lookbook & Gallery (`/lookbook` or `/gallery`)**: Filterable masonry gallery with high-resolution lightbox view.
12. **Contact & Enquiries (`/contact`)**: Multi-tab validated business inquiry portal (General, Wholesale, Private Label, Launch VIP) with direct mailto integration and clipboard copier.
13. **FAQ (`/faq`)**: Categorized animated accordion covering operations, private label MOQs, formulation safety, and launches.
14. **Privacy & Terms (`/privacy`, `/terms`)**: Professional legal and operational disclosures.
15. **404 Not Found (`*`)**: Custom-styled luxury error page.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack & Performance

- **Framework:** React 19 + TypeScript + Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS (Custom luxury ivory, champagne, antique gold, and botanical green palette)
- **Typography:** Google Fonts (`Cormorant Garamond`, `Bodoni Moda`, `Italiana`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`)
- **Animations:** Framer Motion + Lenis Smooth Scrolling (respects `prefers-reduced-motion`)
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Celebration Effects:** Canvas Confetti
- **Performance:** Code-split route architecture with custom preloader, `sitemap.xml`, and `robots.txt`
